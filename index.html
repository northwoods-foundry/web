Sextant Gov™

For municipal government

Sextant Gov turns long-range plans into work an organization can assign, track and report on, organized around the departments, boards and committees that actually do it. Everyone from staff to the public can see what was promised and what's been done.

Sextant Gov grew out of Tim’s service on Winthrop, Maine’s Comprehensive Plan Implementation Committee. Seeing how difficult it was to track progress from a written plan, he built the tool he wished the committee had.

How we protect data

Strong security shouldn’t require an in-house IT department. We build protections into our software from the start. Here’s how we approach it, with Sextant Gov as the example.

Stronger sign-in

We favor modern sign-in, such as passkeys or your organization’s single sign-on, over passwords.

In Sextant Gov: passkeys only, so there’s no password to phish, reuse or leak.

Public and private, by design

We work with you to define what should be public, what must stay private and who can access it, then build those decisions into the software.

In Sextant Gov: the plan is public, but names, emails and phone numbers are hidden from anyone not signed in, and each town’s records are kept separate.

Security from the first line of code

Credentials never live in the code, every change is scanned for leaked secrets before it ships, and dependencies are watched for known vulnerabilities.

In Sextant Gov: uploaded files are checked before use, and changes and access decisions are recorded with who made them.
