How we protect town data

Most of the towns we serve have no IT department, so security is built in rather than left for them to configure.

Passkeys, not passwords

Sign-in uses passkeys only, so there are no passwords to phish, reuse or leak.

Each town kept separate

Every municipality's records are isolated from every other's, enforced on every database query.

Private by default

Names, emails and phone numbers are removed from anything the public can see.

Every upload inspected

Documents are checked for hazards and sensitive content before anything is processed.

Complete audit trail

Changes and access decisions are recorded and kept indefinitely, so a town can always see who did what.

Working toward SOC 2

Code is scanned for secrets and vulnerabilities on every change, and our controls follow the SOC 2 criteria.
